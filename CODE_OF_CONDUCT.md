# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in the CodeQL Extractor Generator
community welcoming and respectful. Treat others with consideration, assume
good intent, and discuss ideas constructively. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces when someone is
representing the project.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior
does not meet these standards. To report a concern, contact
[opensource@github.com](mailto:opensource@github.com). Reports will be handled
as confidentially as possible.
